# PD-005 · SCOPE AND NON-GOALS

**Project ID:** FATHER-ACADEMY-OSINT-001  
**Status:** DRAFT

## MVP IN SCOPE

1. One profession: OSINT Specialist.
2. One student: ALINA_OSINT_STUDENT.
3. One teacher/methodologist role: ALINA.
4. Profession/Competency/Skill model.
5. KnowledgeNode/KnowledgeEdge with provenance.
6. SourceDocument/SourceFragment.
7. Curriculum/Lesson/Assignment.
8. TrainingRun.
9. Evidence.
10. Evaluation.
11. SkillProfile/AgentProfile.
12. Versioned Procedure.
13. One end-to-end assignment: OSINT-TG-001.
14. One correction/retraining loop.
15. One restart/recovery proof.
16. One minimal UI only where it exposes real state.
17. Reuse of existing FATHER/OSINT components only after inventory/validation.

## OUT OF SCOPE

- separate LMS product;
- marketplace of courses;
- multi-tenant production platform;
- commercial billing;
- training hundreds of agents;
- SFT/DPO/RL;
- autonomous high-risk OSINT;
- unrestricted tool access;
- Red Team/pentest autonomy;
- production legal certification of an "OSINT professional";
- replacement of human domain expert;
- final architecture before Pre-Design completion;
- replacement of existing Telegram collector without evidence of need.

## Deferred

- other professions;
- adaptive curriculum optimization;
- automatic skill decay;
- cross-agent peer review;
- team training;
- synthetic training environments;
- SFT/DPO/RL datasets;
- formal professional certification;
- production-grade scalability.
